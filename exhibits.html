Live Exhibitions

Step into the past—today

Our latest exhibition is now live, featuring lifelike robotic creatures brought to life through advanced engineering and real paleontological research. Come face-to-face with moving, roaring prehistoric giants, explore interactive displays, and discover how science and imagination revive the ancient world in stunning detail.

Reign of the Spinosaurus

Towering over its surroundings with a sail-like spine and crocodile-like jaws, the Spinosaurus is a marvel of modern robotics and prehistoric science. Cutting-edge mechanics and informed by the latest fossil findings, this aquatic apex predator moves with lifelike precision—snapping, stepping, and stirring the imagination.

Paraceratherium: The Gentle Giant

One of the largest land mammals to ever walk the Earth. Towering over modern elephants, the Paraceratherium combines massive scale with smooth, lifelike movement—bringing this long-extinct giant back to life. Crafted for wonder and accuracy, it’s a peaceful reminder of just how colossal prehistoric life could be.

Arthropleura: The Titan of the Forest Floor

Before dinosaurs ruled the land, this giant millipede-like creature crawled through prehistoric forests. The Arthropleura stretches over 8 feet long, with articulated segments that slither like the real thing. Creepy, crawly, and completely captivating—this ancient invertebrate proves that not all giants walked on four legs.

Upcoming Exhibitions

Travel back in time—no time machine needed

Archelix invites you to experience our newest exhibition, featuring lifelike robotic creatures powered by cutting-edge engineering and real paleontology. Get up close with roaring, moving prehistoric giants, explore interactive displays, and see how science brings ancient life back to the modern world.

Titanoboa: The Monster Beneath the Marsh

Long before snakes slithered through jungles, Titanoboa ruled the prehistoric swamps. Stretching over 40 feet, the Archelix Titanoboa coils and moves with eerie realism—recreating the silent power of Earth’s largest known snake. An engineering feat and a paleontological terror, this serpent will leave you breathless.

Meganeura: Wings of the Ancient Sky

With a wingspan over two feet wide, Meganeura soared through Carboniferous skies like a dragonfly on steroids. At Archelix, we’ve brought this prehistoric predator to life with delicate wing movements and astonishing detail. Light as air, this ancient insect reminds us how fearsome even the smallest creatures were.

Argentavis: The Sky’s Original Giant

With wings stretching over 20 feet, Argentavis was one of the largest flying birds to ever exist. At Archelix, we’ve recreated this majestic predator mid-flight—complete with powerful wing beats and piercing gaze. Soaring above the rest, Argentavis brings the ancient skies to life like never before.
